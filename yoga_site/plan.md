# Building and hosting a yoga class website

For a yoga class site, you don't need anything complicated. A simple static site (plain HTML/CSS) is fast, cheap, and can be hosted for free.

## Step 1: Plan your content
A good yoga site usually has these sections (all can live on one scrolling page):
- **Hero:** your name or studio, a tagline, and a "Book a free trial" button
- **About you:** photo, certifications, teaching style
- **Classes:** type (Hatha, Vinyasa, prenatal, etc.), level, and online or in-person
- **Schedule and pricing**
- **Testimonials:** a few student quotes
- **Contact:** WhatsApp button, phone, Instagram, Google Maps location

Collect 5-10 good photos too. They matter more than anything else on a wellness site.
